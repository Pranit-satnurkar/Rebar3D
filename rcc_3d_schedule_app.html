Project summary

Stock-bar cutting plan

First-fit decreasing estimate using 12,000 mm stock bars. Saw kerf and splice design are not included.

Steel mass = 0.00617d² kg/m (IS 1786). Stirrup/tie cutting length = 2[(b−2c)+(D−2c)] + 2×10d (135° hooks, min 75 mm) − 4×2d (90° bends) − 2×3d (135° bends), per IS 2502 / SP 34. Beam bars = L − 2c + 2 × extra anchorage; layer 2 bars sit at a clear gap of max(25 mm, bar dia); 4-leg stirrups use an inner stirrup of half the outer width; column bars = H + lap; no deduction for bars from concrete (IS 1200 Part 2). Shuttering = contact area (IS 1200 Part 5); a monolithic slab depth reduces beam depth for both. Wastage: concrete 2.5%, steel 5%, shown separately. Checks are detailing checks assuming Fe500, not structural design. Confirm clause numbers against your copy of IS 456:2000.
